Concha de Palacio / Alena Studio

Designer bags
with identity.

San Diego, California

Alena Studio creates contemporary bags and accessories as design objects, balancing precise silhouettes, considered materials and a deeply personal point of view.

Objects made
to be lived with.

Each piece begins with proportion, function and character. The collection explores form and material through accessories designed to accompany everyday life without losing their individuality.

As part of the Concha de Palacio creative house, Alena Studio shares a sensitivity for lasting details, tactile materials and objects that remain relevant beyond a single season.
