Concha de Palacio / Interiors

Interior design
with emotion.

San Diego, California

Concha de Palacio designs residential and hospitality interiors through a considered dialogue between light, art, material and the way each space is lived.

Spaces with
a point of view.

Every project begins with context: the architecture, the people who inhabit it and the atmosphere the space should create. We seek coherence rather than formula, bringing together proportion, texture, objects and art.

The result is an interior with identity—refined but personal, expressive yet enduring—developed for residential and hospitality projects in San Diego and beyond.
