Concha de Palacio / NODÉ

Artisan jewelry,
individually felt.

San Diego, California

NODÉ creates handcrafted necklaces, pearl jewelry and expressive pieces that honor material, gesture and the beauty of imperfection.

Made by hand.
Worn personally.

Each piece is approached as a small composition. Pearls, texture and proportion come together in jewelry designed to feel individual rather than uniform.

NODÉ belongs to the Concha de Palacio creative universe, sharing its attention to emotion, craftsmanship and objects capable of forming a lasting connection with the person who wears them.
