6. Rights in Mexico

Under Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, you may exercise rights of access, rectification, cancellation and objection (ARCO), withdraw consent or limit the use and disclosure of your data. Email your request to contact@conchadepalacio.com with your name, response method, right requested, description of the data and proof of identity or authority. We will respond within the statutory periods. You may also contact Mexico's Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Government.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection; withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing; and complain to the supervisory authority where you live, work or where the alleged infringement occurred. You may also request information about safeguards used for international transfers.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising and do not use sensitive information for purposes that trigger the right to limit. Use the contact details in section 1 to exercise these rights. We may verify your identity before responding. An authorised agent may submit a request with appropriate documentation.
